PhD opportunity: Engineering biology to modulate toxin–antitoxin dynamics and multidrug resistance

We are advertising a PhD project investigating how engineering biology can be used to study toxin–antitoxin dynamics and the stability of multidrug resistance in important foodborne pathogens. The project brings together engineering biology and microbiology to investigate how one of the biggests driver's of success of multidrug resistance plasmids can be flipped into an Achille's Heel!

Application deadline: Midday (GMT), Wednesday 2 December 2026.
