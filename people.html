Principal Investigator

Rob Habgood

Lecturer in Biotechnology · Department of Life Sciences · University of Bath

Rob is a synthetic biology researcher who obtained his PhD at the University of Nottingham's Synthetic Biology Research Centre in 2018. Following his PhD, he was a PDRA at the University of Oxford and then Head of Research at the agritech startup Folium Science.

Rob joined the University of Bath as a Lecturer in Biotechnology in 2026 and is now establishing his own research group in microbiome editing.
