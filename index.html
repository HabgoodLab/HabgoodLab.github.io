Research

Engineering biology at the level of microbial communities.

Our work sits at the intersection of synthetic biology, microbiome science and One Health. We are interested in how editing tools can be deployed in complex biological systems, what happens to edited microbes after intervention, and how microbial gene transfer shapes these systems.

Biological Control

Developing biological approaches for controlling important microbes.

Principal Investigator

Rob Habgood

Lecturer in Biotechnology

Rob is a synthetic biology researcher working at the intersection of engineering biology and microbial systems. He joined the University of Bath in 2026 and is establishing the Habgood Lab to develop new approaches to microbiome editing and the engineering of microbial systems.
